--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Customer creates reservation → Status: PENDING
2. Staff approves → Status: APPROVED (email sent)
3. Customer clicks confirm in email → Status: CONFIRMED (table blocked)
4. After event, staff completes → Status: COMPLETED (table freed)

Alternative: Can be CANCELLED at any time before completion

Key Innovation: Only CONFIRMED reservations block tables
(APPROVED and PENDING don't hold tables)
